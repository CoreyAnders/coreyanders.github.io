welcome to the jungle

An old bamboo cutter found a tiny child inside a stalk filled with light. He carried her home, believing the discovery to be a blessing.

She entered the world as a gift, though the world would soon mistake a gift for a possession.

What the story leaves behind

Love does not grant ownership. Some things remain precious precisely because they cannot be kept.

The suitors offered status, imitation, and deception. The Emperor offered power. Her family offered devotion. None of it could turn belonging into possession.
